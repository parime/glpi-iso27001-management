### Review a risk, check the effectiveness of its treatment and sign its acceptance

An auditor asks for evidence that risk treatment is effective and that residual risk is accepted by
an authorised person (clauses 6.1.3, 8.3 and 9.1). On a risk form:

1. Choose the **Target risk level**, the level the treatment must reach. Once every action of the
   treatment plan is completed, the **Effectiveness of the treatment** field shows **Target
   reached**, or an alert **Plan completed, target not reached** if the residual risk is still
   above it (add actions or record a decision). The dashboard card **Treatment plans completed
   without reaching the target** counts these risks.
2. Open the **Risk reviews** tab: write the **Finding of the review**, choose the **Next review
   date** and click **Record the review**. The assessment of the day is kept with the review; the
   curve shows how the initial and residual levels evolve from one review to the next, with the
   target. A recorded review can no longer be changed.
3. For an **Accepted** risk, the **Acceptance of the risk** field lets you choose the **Acceptance
   valid until** date and tick **Sign the acceptance**, then save. The owner of the risk can never
   sign: ask the risk manager, the risk committee or management. When the expiry date gets close,
   tick **Renew the acceptance**; the **Risk acceptances to renew** card lists them.

To hand out these roles, go to **Setup > Plugins > GRC Manager**, **Risks** tab, block
**Ready-made risk management profiles**: each profile is created in one click with only what it
needs (internal audit is read only, no profile reaches the assets), then assigned from the user
record.
