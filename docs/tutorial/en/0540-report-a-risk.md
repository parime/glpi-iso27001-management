### Let staff report risks, then triage them

Staff often notice risks before the IT team does: a door that no longer locks, a password shown on
a screen, a supplier in trouble. The plugin lets them report these without opening the risk
register to them (clause 6.1.2, control A.6.8):

1. In the simplified interface, the employee clicks the **GRC Manager** menu entry (or, in the
   standard interface, the **My risk reports** tab of their preferences).
2. They say **what they noticed** (a short title), **describe the situation** and choose the
   **seriousness that seems right** to them ("I don't know" is accepted), then click **Send my
   report**.
3. Below the form they only find their own reports, with their state: "Received, waiting to be
   examined", "Taken into account" or "Not kept", and the answer of the person in charge of risks.

The person in charge of risks opens **Risks > Reported risks**. Reports to triage come first, the
most serious ones on top. For each one:

- **Accept** creates an "identified" risk with the title and description of the report, in the
  author's entity, then opens it to complete the owner, likelihood and impact;
- **Link** attaches the report to a risk that is already known, without changing that risk;
- **Reject** requires a reason, which the author will read.

The decision is final. The author receives a notification, and the **Reported risks** tab of each
risk shows the auditor the reports that created it or were linked to it. The queue only requires
reading the risk register; triaging requires the right to create and update risks.
